Built different

Hive doesn't bolt a chatbot onto a browser. It makes the browser itself the memory, the research tool, and the assistant.

Memory that acts

Capture any page in one click — no screen recording, no account. Pages you capture become stored, citable sources your AI answers from.

Cited answers

Ask "what was I reading about X?" and Hive answers with real citations to the pages you actually opened, never generated labels.

On-device AI

Models run locally on Apple Silicon via MLX. No internet needed. Your data never leaves your machine.

Deep research

Plan → search → read → synthesize → cite. Multi-step research with inline source links and honest model labels.

Workspaces

Per-workspace cookie and storage isolation. Separate work, personal, and research contexts with one click.

Built-in adblocking

The Brave adblock-rust engine with network-level filtering and cosmetic blocking, out of the box.

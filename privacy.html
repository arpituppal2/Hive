Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Our Promise

Hive is built on the principle that your browsing data belongs to you. We collect nothing by default. No telemetry. No analytics. No tracking.

What Stays On Your Device

  • Browsing history — stored locally, never uploaded
  • Bookmarks and passwords — encrypted in your Keychain
  • AI model inference — runs on-device via MLX (Apple Silicon)
  • Tab state and sessions — local SQLite, crash-only persistence

What May Leave Your Device

  • Safe Browsing — 4-byte hash prefixes sent to Google (same as Chrome). Full URLs never leave your device.
  • Search queries — sent to your chosen search engine (DuckDuckGo by default, Google/Bing optional)
  • Cloud Sync (opt-in) — encrypted via CloudKit. Your data is E2E encrypted; Apple cannot read it.
  • BYOK AI providers — if you configure your own API key, queries go to that provider under their privacy policy.
  • Auto-updates — Sparkle checks for new versions via your configured feed URL.

What We Never Do

  • ❌ Sell your data
  • ❌ Track you across sites
  • ❌ Log your browsing
  • ❌ Share data with third parties
  • ❌ Use your content to train models
